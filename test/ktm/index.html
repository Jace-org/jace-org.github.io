About the Bikram Sambat date

The Bikram Sambat (BS) calendar is Nepal's official calendar. Its month lengths (29 to 32 days) are fixed by published tables, not by a formula, so this page carries a small table for BS years 2080 to 2085. That covers 14 April 2023 to 13 April 2029; outside it the page says "out of range" instead of guessing. The table was checked against the new-year dates 14 April 2025 (1 Baisakh 2082) and 14 April 2026 (1 Baisakh 2083). Use an official panchang for anything legal or religious: some tables differ by a day in rare years.
