Before it shakes

Nepal sits on an active plate boundary, so strong earthquakes will keep happening and this page only records events that already took place. It does not forecast anything, and no tool can say when the next one comes.

  • Drop, cover, hold on. Get under a sturdy table or against an inside wall, away from windows and heavy shelves.
  • Know your exit. Agree on an open assembly point with your household. Avoid lifts and stairs during shaking.
  • Prepare a bag. Water, torch, radio, first-aid kit, copies of key documents and a charged power bank.
  • Secure what can fall. Strap tall furniture, keep heavy items low, know where your gas and mains switch are.
  • Follow official guidance. Nepal's National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority and the Department of Mines and Geology publish local advice.

Data sources & privacy

Quake data is fetched from the USGS Earthquake Hazards Program (public domain). The map uses Leaflet 1.9.4 from cdnjs and tiles from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Your browser contacts those three services directly, so each sees your IP address. Nothing is sent to this site's servers or stored.
