Honeypot Lab

Poke at the whole idea in your browser: fire attacks at a decoy, watch them get classified, work the cases in a management portal, then see each weakness found, fixed and retested.

Simulation. Everything here runs locally in this page with made-up data and documentation-range addresses. Nothing is sent anywhere, and no real portal exists yet: this is a mock-up of the planned one. See the project overview.
