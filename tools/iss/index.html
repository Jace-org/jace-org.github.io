Where it is

Yellow dot: the station now. Faint line: its path from 30 minutes ago to 60 minutes ahead. White dot: Kathmandu. The map is a plain latitude and longitude grid, not a land map. Position updates every 10 seconds while this tab is visible and pauses when you switch away.

Data sources & privacy

Positions come from the Where the ISS at? public API. Your browser contacts it directly, so it sees your IP address. Nothing is sent to this site's servers or stored. Distances are computed in your browser; "straight line" includes the station's altitude, "along the ground" does not.
