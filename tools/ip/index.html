IP and network owner

Who owns this IP?

Enter an IP, a prefix or an AS number and see who runs it and whether its route is signed.

Data sources & privacy

What you type is sent to RIPEstat (stat.ripe.net, run by the RIPE NCC) to look up the prefix, the AS holder and the RPKI validation. RIPEstat sees your IP address like any website you visit.

Your own IP is fetched only if you click "Use my own IP"; that click asks RIPEstat what address it sees you as. Nothing is stored and nothing goes to this site. Private and reserved addresses are rejected before any request.
