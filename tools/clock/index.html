Kathmandu in one line

A tiny header widget that shows Nepal time and the temperature, written as a snippet you can drop into any page.

In the header

J.K Home   Blog   Tech

The line above is the real widget. If the weather service does not answer within 8 seconds, it quietly drops to just "22:58 in Kathmandu".

Use it

One small file, no dependencies. Copy ktm-clock.js next to the page, add the three lines below where the clock should appear, and style .ktm-clock to fit the header. The element stays hidden until the script has a time to show.

<span id="ktm-clock" class="ktm-clock" hidden></span>
<script src="ktm-clock.js"></script>
<script>ktmClock(document.getElementById('ktm-clock'));</script>

What it does: the time is formatted in your browser for the Asia/Kathmandu zone (UTC+5:45, no daylight saving). The temperature is fetched once at load and every 15 minutes, rounded to a whole degree, and validated as a plain number in a sane range before it is shown. It only ever writes with textContent.

Data sources & privacy

The time is computed in your browser. The temperature comes from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), which sees your IP address when the widget loads. Nothing is sent to this site's servers or stored.
