    EPSS vs CVSS

    CVSS says how bad a flaw could be if it is exploited: a technical severity from 0 to 10. EPSS says how likely exploitation is in the next 30 days: a probability from 0 to 100%, re-estimated daily from real-world signals. A flaw can be a 9.8 on CVSS and still sit at 0.1% on EPSS, and a modest 6.5 can carry a 60% chance. Use CVSS for impact, EPSS for urgency, and look at both.

    Brand-new CVEs often have no EPSS score yet, so they show as "not scored yet" rather than a guess.

    Data sources & privacy

    This page loads the latest CVE records from CIRCL (cve.circl.lu) and their scores from FIRST (api.first.org). Both services see your IP address when the page loads. No input of yours is sent anywhere.

    EPSS is published by FIRST, the CVE data comes from CIRCL's public feed. All values are treated as untrusted and shown as plain text. Nothing is stored. A probability is a forecast, not a verdict.
